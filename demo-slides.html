The problem Demo deck · Not final

Crew health can’t wait on Earth

On a Mars transit, one-way light delay is ~11+ minutes. Crews need onboard triage before a flight surgeon can reply.

Delay

Earth advice arrives late. Local decisions still need structure.

Wearables

Bands and vests stream noisy biometrics — someone has to watch them.

Scope

Tools must recommend actions, not invent diagnoses or prescriptions.
